## Narration and shots

### 0:00–0:25 — The freelancer's decision

Shot: app title and synthetic order card. Narration:

“You have finished a freelance project. Your client says the payment is approved.
Can you safely release the work? Approval is one step; it is not evidence that
funds were captured. PayProof helps you answer the practical question: what does
the evidence actually let me conclude?”

### 0:25–0:55 — Approval versus paid

Shot: visibly labeled synthetic approval scenario, then synthetic paid scenario.
Narration:

“This first view is an offline simulation. I can compare approval with the paid
scenario and see why the conclusion changes. These are demonstration fixtures,
not money moving through PayPal. The interface keeps the evidence state visible
instead of turning a reassuring label into a promise that someone has been paid.”

### 0:55–1:20 — Conflict and a useful pause

Shot: synthetic conflict and unavailable scenarios; show explanation, not raw JSON.
Narration:

“Now the evidence conflicts, or a fresh answer is unavailable. PayProof refuses
to invent completion. It tells me to reconcile the evidence rather than repeat a
financial operation. That pause is useful: a freelancer can investigate before
releasing a deliverable, and a client can see why the status still needs attention.”

### 1:20–2:00 — An actual result, honestly recorded

Shot: separate Recorded Sandbox117 panel and its provenance disclosure.
Narration:

“Here is a different kind of evidence: a saved result from one real Sandbox read

Shot: recorded advisory fields and compact explanation. Narration:
